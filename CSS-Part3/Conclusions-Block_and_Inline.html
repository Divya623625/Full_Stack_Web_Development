Point 3: Margins work horizontally, but not vertically - Left and right margins work. - Top and bottom margins do not affect the normal layout of surrounding elements. Point 4: Padding works on all sides, but top and bottom may overlap Padding means the space inside an element. Inline elements accept padding on all four sides. However, top and bottom padding may overlap other elements because they don't increase the normal line layout height.
